# Monetization Playbook - dream-interpretation-pages

This file is a commercial planning artifact, not a guarantee of revenue. Use it to make the repository legible as a sellable product, pilot, workshop, license, or proof-of-value package.

## Commercial Lane

- **Lane:** B2C consumer AI and ads funnel
- **Primary buyer:** Consumer AI users, content communities, and SEO-driven entertainment/reflection traffic.
- **Revenue wedge:** Cloudflare Pages app with safe fallback, lightweight personalization, and monetizable content paths.

## Offer Ladder

1. **Ad-supported content site** - pricing hypothesis: Ads + affiliate
2. **Paid interpretation packs** - pricing hypothesis: $3-$9 paid pack
3. **Theme/history sync subscription** - pricing hypothesis: $4-$8/month sync

## Acquisition Channels

- SEO symbol library
- Short-form content
- Community prompt templates

## Proof Gates Before Selling

- npm run verify passes
- Fallback works without keys
- Abuse controls are reviewable

## Risk Boundaries

- Entertainment only
- Privacy and abuse controls needed
- AdSense requires original content depth

## 30/60/90 Day GTM

- **30 days:** Publish 50 original symbol pages
- **60 days:** Add email capture
- **90 days:** Test paid pack landing

## Metrics That Matter

- Organic visits
- Interpretation completion
- Pack conversion

## Positioning Note

Lead with the specific operational pain, then show the runnable proof, then offer a narrow pilot. Do not pitch the repository as a vague app; pitch it as a concrete workflow with evidence, risk boundaries, and a next buying step.
