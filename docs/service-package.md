# Service Package - dream-interpretation-pages

This package defines the most practical service shape for this repository. It is public-safe: no price promises, financial projections, or buyer-specific contract assumptions are published here.

## Buyer and Wedge

- **Service lane:** Consumer content surface
- **Primary buyer:** Search-driven visitors seeking lightweight interpretation or reflective content
- **Front-door offer:** Policy-safe content and ads readiness
- **Service outcome:** Package content quality, disclaimers, analytics, and ad-policy readiness before monetization.

## Build and Polish Priorities

- Improve content structure and safety boundaries
- Separate entertainment/reflection from claims
- Add analytics and content governance checklist

## Buyer-Ready Deliverables

- Content audit
- Policy review
- SEO page polish

## Activation Path

1. Use the public demo, README, screenshots, and tests to establish proof.
2. Confirm the buyer workflow, data boundary, owner, and acceptance criteria.
3. Build only the smallest workflow needed to prove the service outcome.
4. Add cloud resources, accounts, storage, or paid integrations only after the workflow boundary is approved.

## Margin and Risk Controls

- Keep hosting static and enable ads only after policy review and traffic quality are validated.
- Keep the first service scope narrow, repeatable, and evidence-led.
- Keep private financial planning outside the public repository.
- Keep logs, analytics, and reports free of private payloads unless explicit approval exists.

## Portfolio Signal

The portfolio should present this repository as **Policy-safe content and ads readiness** under the **Consumer content surface** lane, with proof links routed to the live demo or repository evidence.
